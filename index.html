What I have built

Projects that start with a real problem

Featured project

Rozgar Hub

روزگار

AI-Powered Employment Solutions

Turning AI into practical opportunities.

The problem

Early in the morning, workers gather at local labour points hoping to find daily-wage work. Finding a job this way is informal, uncertain, and hard to trust for both workers and employers.

The solution

Rozgar Hub is an AI-assisted employment platform for workers and employers in Pakistan. It combines AI-based job matching, natural language processing, location-based discovery, user profiles, job posting, communication, ratings, and payment verification. It works in English and Urdu, so workers can use it in their own language.

My role

I worked on key parts of the system: Google authentication, CNIC-based profiles, NLP functionality, GPS and location features, and the integration of AI services.
